# Demo recording

`worksight-demo.mp4` (3:40, 1920×1080, H.264/AAC, about 19 MB) is the submission video. It is one
continuous edit of title cards in the style of the pitch deck and screen recordings of the live site
(https://web-production-d9743.up.railway.app), recorded with Playwright on 18 October 2026:
welcome tutorial and languages, one-click inspection of the bent-pins board, heatmap / compare /
original and zoom, the saved Gemma description, the engineer review, a good board, History, batch
inspection and part documents.

The voice-over is synthesized (Microsoft Edge neural voice "Andrew") from the script in
`demo-script.md`; `worksight-demo.srt` has the same text as subtitles. Every number it says comes
from `docs/BENCHMARK.md`. Re-record with a team member's voice if preferred: the script and timing
are the same.

The old silent `worksight-demo.webm` (synthetic demo-mode UI) was removed.
